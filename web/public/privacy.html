Porkour – Privacy policy

Porkour is a free browser game. This page explains what it does with your data. Last update: 2026-11-13.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the game receives your name, profile picture and a Google account id. They are used only to show who you are to the other players in your room. You can always play as a guest instead.

What is stored

  • Your settings, outfit and guest name are saved in your own browser (local storage).
  • The game server keeps your name and picture only while you are connected. Nothing is sold or shared, and there are no ads or trackers.
  • If player progress (coins, items) is added later, it will be stored only to give it back to you, and this page will be updated.

Online play

To play together, browsers exchange game data directly (peer-to-peer) or through the game server. Other players in your room can see your name and picture.

Contact / delete

Questions or deletion requests: github.com/ohavs/Porkour/issues. To remove local data, clear this site's data in your browser.

Porkour – Política de privacidad

Porkour es un juego gratuito en el navegador. Si inicias sesión con Google (opcional), el juego recibe tu nombre, tu foto de perfil y un identificador de cuenta, solo para mostrar quién eres a los demás jugadores de tu sala. Puedes jugar como invitado. Tus ajustes, tu ropa y tu nombre de invitado se guardan en tu navegador. El servidor guarda tu nombre y foto solo mientras estás conectado. No vendemos ni compartimos datos, sin anuncios ni rastreadores. Preguntas o borrado: github.com/ohavs/Porkour/issues.
